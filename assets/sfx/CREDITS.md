Freesound sounds are the site's public high-quality previews of CC0 uploads (raw copies in `assets-src/sfx/freesound/`,
with `sources.json`). Which recording each sound uses, and where in it, is in `tools/sfx/import_cc0.py` (`GUNS`,
`VOICES`, `FOLEY`, `AMBIENCE`). Anything not in `manifest.json` (dry fire, the crossbow and launchers, your grunt, the echo off the
houses, the Pack-a-Punch layer) is synthesized in `src/audio/`.
